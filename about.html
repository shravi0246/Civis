ABOUT PROJECT

About CivicShield

CivicShield aims to provide a simple and user-friendly platform where citizens can report incidents, access emergency contacts, and track complaint progress.

Why CivicShield?

🔹 Easy crime and incident reporting
🔹 Quick access to emergency helplines
🔹 Complaint tracking and status updates
🔹 Clean and beginner-friendly interface
🔹 Focus on public awareness and safety

In many communities, incident reporting can be confusing or intimidating. CivicShield aims to simplify the entire process by offering a clear interface where anyone can submit a complaint, track progress, or access emergency support numbers without complications.

Clean Code

Built with beginner-friendly HTML, CSS, and minimal JavaScript ideal for college academic demonstrations.

Fully Responsive

Designed to render cleanly across desktops, tablets, and smartphones with mobile hamburger navigation.
