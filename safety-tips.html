CITIZEN AWARENESS

Crime Prevention & Safety Tips

Stay aware, secure your surroundings, and know how to protect yourself and your family with these practical safety guidelines.

Personal & Street Safety

  • Stay Alert: Avoid looking down at your phone while walking through deserted areas at night.
  • Well-Lit Routes: Stick to busy, well-illuminated streets rather than taking isolated shortcuts.
  • Emergency Numbers: Keep primary contacts on speed-dial on your mobile phone.
  • Trust Your Instincts: If you feel uncomfortable or followed, head towards the nearest public store.

Cybercrime & Online Fraud

  • Never Share OTP: Police and bank officials will never ask for your confidential one-time passwords.
  • Verify Links: Check URLs before clicking on urgent SMS or email lottery claims.
  • Strong Passwords: Use unique, complex passwords for your bank and email accounts.
  • Immediate Action: Report financial scams within 24 hours to freeze suspicious transactions.

Home & Neighborhood Security

  • Secure Locks: Double check deadbolts on entry doors and ground-floor windows before sleeping.
  • Adequate Lighting: Maintain bright motion-detector lighting around gates and driveway areas.
  • Know Neighbors: Share contacts with trusted neighbors to watch over homes during vacations.
  • Report Suspicious Persons: Log unknown persons loitering around residential homes on CivicShield.

Emergency Preparedness

  • Know Helplines: Memorize Police (100 / 911), Ambulance (102), and Unified Emergency (112).
  • Medical Info: Keep an ID card with your blood group and emergency contact in your wallet.
  • Safe Meeting Point: Agree on a family rendezvous point in case of neighborhood crises.
  • Document Incidents: Note time, vehicle registration numbers, and visual descriptions accurately.
