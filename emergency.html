EMERGENCY ASSISTANCE

Emergency Helplines

If you or someone around you is in immediate danger, please dial an emergency helpline number right away.

Critical Emergency Notice

Online forms are for non-urgent crime reporting and records. For active crimes, violent threats, or medical emergencies, call the numbers below immediately.

Police Control Room

For instant armed response, theft in progress, or immediate protection.

Call 100 / 911

Medical Ambulance

For urgent medical assistance, serious injuries, or accidents.

Call 102

Women Safety Helpline

24/7 dedicated helpline for women safety, distress, and harassment.

Call 1091

National Emergency Number

Unified all-in-one emergency service for police, fire, and ambulance.

Call 112
