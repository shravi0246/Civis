EMERGENCY ASSISTANCE

Emergency Helplines

If you or someone around you is in immediate danger, please dial an emergency helpline number right away.

Critical Emergency Notice

Online forms are for non-urgent crime reporting and records. For active crimes, violent threats, or medical emergencies, call the numbers below immediately.

Police Control Room Urgent

For instant armed police response, theft in progress, violence, or immediate personal safety protection.

Call 100 / 911

Medical Ambulance Hospital

For urgent medical emergencies, serious physical injuries, road accidents, and paramedic dispatch.

Call 102

Women Safety Helpline 24/7 Helpline

Dedicated round-the-clock helpline for women in distress, stalking, harassment, or domestic threat situations.

Call 1091

National Emergency Number Unified 112

Single integrated emergency response system connecting police, fire services, and medical dispatch.

Call 112

Cyber Crime Helpline Financial Fraud

Immediate reporting of unauthorized bank debit, UPI fraud, phishing scams, and digital identity blackmail.

Call 1930

Fire & Rescue Services Fire Safety

Emergency fire suppression, building rescue, gas leaks, and industrial hazard containment.

Call 101

Childline Assistance Child Safety

24-hour national emergency outreach service for children in need of care, rescue, and protection.

Call 1098

Senior Citizen Support Elder Help

Dedicated elder care, distress protection, abuse reporting, and rapid emergency police welfare assistance.

Call 14567
